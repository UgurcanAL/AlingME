# Lab-animal science / 3Rs / animal-ethics journals: which ones would publish "Two Gazettes" (EU Directive 2010/63/EU vs Turkish law) at zero cost to the author (checked 28 Sep 2026)

Scope: a comparative regulatory/legal analysis of about 10,000 words, written in English, by an author in Türkiye who will not pay anything.
Method: I read official publisher/journal pages where the fetch tools reached them (Firecrawl scrapes of SAGE, Cambridge, ALTEX and KGL/AALAS pages). Where I could only see search-engine excerpts of official pages, the note says "(excerpt)". Direct fetches of nature.com and ojs.utlib.ee failed (proxy/classifier errors), so the Lab Animal and SJLAS facts come from excerpts of their official pages. Items I could not verify are marked UNVERIFIED.

## Q1. Fees: mandatory APC, free subscription route, submission, page and colour charges

### Takeaway
These journals are genuinely free to publish in:
- **ATLA** and **Laboratory Animals** (SAGE subscription route)
- **JAALAS / Comparative Medicine** (AALAS)
- **Laboratory Animal Research** (the society covers the APC)
- **Research Ethics** (SAGE Community Open Access)
- **Animal Sentience**
- **Lab Animal**, on its subscription route only.

These charge the author:
- **ALTEX**: a €2,500 APC. Waivers exist only when an institutional official co-signs a request.
- **Animal Welfare** (UFAW/CUP): an APC of £2,610 / $3,655. It is waived for Commentaries and similar editorial items. Otherwise a waiver needs a Research4Life country (Türkiye does not qualify) or an ad-hoc waiver requested at or before submission.
- **SJLAS is NOT free diamond OA** for non-members. It charges €250 per accepted manuscript unless the corresponding author has been a Scand-LAS member for at least one year.

### Cited Findings

**ATLA (SAGE / FRAME)**
- The fees statement: "There are no fees payable to submit or publish in this journal." Colour figures are free online; colour in print costs extra only if the author requests it. Open access is optional and paid (Sage Choice). [SAGE ATLA submission guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/atl) (full section shown in search excerpt)

**Laboratory Animals (SAGE / LAL)**
- Key information lists "Access: Subscription".
- The fees statement: "There are no fees payable to submit or publish in this journal."
- Open access is optional, for a fee, via Sage Choice.
- Colour is free online. Colour in print is charged only if the author requests it ("we will advise you of the costs").
- Source for all of the above: [SAGE LAN submission guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN) (full page scraped)

**ALTEX (ALTEX Edition): fully OA and charges an APC**
- "ALTEX will bill authors an article processing charge of €2,500 per full manuscript (Food for Thought ... articles, research articles, reviews, consensus reports, workshop reports, BenchMarks contributions and AOP reports) and €1,300 per short communication upon acceptance." [ALTEX Submissions](https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/about/submissions)
- Letters and meeting reports cost €90 per printed page. "Corners" cost €400 for non-members of ALTEX Edition. Institutional members get a 10% discount. [ALTEX Submissions](https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/about/submissions)
- Waiver: "If your organization has no means to cover the publication costs, please state this in the cover letter and ALTEX may choose to waive these. The request for waiver must be co-signed by an institutional official certifying that the authors do not have funds available." [ALTEX Submissions](https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/about/submissions)
- A waiver is discretionary ("may"), so ALTEX is not zero-cost by default.

**Animal Welfare (UFAW / Science for Animal Welfare, Cambridge University Press): fully gold OA**
- APC: £2,610 / US$3,655. Member rate: £2,090 / $2,925, which reflects a 20% discount for Science for Animal Welfare members. [CUP Animal Welfare Fees and pricing](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing) (scraped)
- "Editorial waivers: There are no APCs for the following article types: Editorials, Letters to the Editor, Article Commentaries, Book Reviews." [same page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing)
- Other routes to a waiver, all on the [same page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing):
  - A CUP Transformative Agreement.
  - A Research4Life country: Group A gets a full waiver, Group B gets 50% off.
  - An ad-hoc waiver "in rare cases when authors and their institutes can clearly demonstrate inability to pay". This must be requested "at or before submission" via a form, and editors are not involved in the request.
- The page says nothing about UFAW covering APCs beyond the member discount. [same page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing)
- The journal moved to CUP and full OA in 2023. [InPublishing](https://www.inpublishing.co.uk/articles/animal-welfare-journal-moves-to-cup-in-2023-20914); [CUP OA options](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/journal-policies/open-access-options) (excerpt)

**Lab Animal (Nature Portfolio): hybrid**
- "Authors who publish open access in Lab Animal are required to pay an article processing charge (APC)… Authors can also choose to publish under the traditional publishing model (no APC charges apply)." [Lab Animal Guide to Authors](https://www.nature.com/laban/authors-and-referees/gta) (excerpt via search)
- The same excerpt gives the OA APC as £3,990 / $5,590 / €4,790. I could not fetch the page directly. [Lab Animal GTA](https://www.nature.com/laban/authors-and-referees/gta)
- The homepage mentions both the "subscription route" and OA via Transformative Agreements. [Lab Animal home](https://www.nature.com/laban/) (excerpt)

**JAALAS and Comparative Medicine (AALAS)**
- The Information for Authors states: "No submission fee. No page charges. No color charges." [AALAS Information for Authors, JAALAS 63(1):99](https://aalas.kglmeridian.com/view/journals/72010024/63/1/article-p99.xml); [PMC copy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10078928/) (excerpts)
- One set of instructions covers both CM and JAALAS. Articles become free to the public 6 months after publication. [AALAS IFA](https://aalas.kglmeridian.com/view/journals/72010024/63/1/article-p99.xml) (scraped)

**Laboratory Animal Research (KALAS, Springer Nature / BMC)**
- "There is no fee to publish in Laboratory Animal Research because the article processing charge (APC) is covered by the Korean Association for Laboratory Animal Science (KALAS)." [Springer LAR submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/42826/submission-guidelines); [How to publish](https://link.springer.com/journal/42826/how-to-publish-with-us)
- DOAJ: "There are no publication fees." Licence is CC BY. [DOAJ 2233-7660](https://doaj.org/toc/2233-7660)

**Scandinavian Journal of Laboratory Animal Science (SJLAS, Scand-LAS)**
- The official submissions page, as seen through search excerpts, says:
  - "Publication is free for submissions where at least the corresponding author is a Scand-LAS member" who has been a member for at least one year.
  - "For non-Scand-LAS members, the fee is 250 € for an accepted manuscript."
  - "There is no handling fee."
  - [SJLAS Submissions](https://ojs.utlib.ee/index.php/SJLAS/about/submissions) (excerpt; direct fetch failed)
- This contradicts the brief's "reportedly free diamond OA".
- The Norwegian Kanalregister lists it as "Not indexed in DOAJ". [Kanalregisteret](https://kanalregister.hkdir.no/en/tidsskrift?id=341555)

**Research Ethics (SAGE)**
- "Research Ethics is a Community Open Access title which means there is no article processing charge (APC) to pay…" [SAGE REA guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/rea) (excerpt)

**Animal Sentience (WellBeing International)**
- "There are no publication fees (article processing charges or APCs)." [DOAJ 2377-7478](https://doaj.org/toc/2377-7478)
- Publisher repository page: [WBI](https://www.wellbeingintlstudiesrepository.org/animsent/)

**dA. Derecho Animal – Forum of Animal Law Studies (UAB / ICALP)**
- It has been open access under CC BY 4.0 since 2017 and is free to read. [DA about](https://revistes.uab.cat/da/about) (excerpt); [DA submissions](https://revistes.uab.cat/da/about/submissions)
- No explicit author-fee statement was retrieved (UNVERIFIED; see Gaps).

**Journal of Applied Animal Ethics Research (Brill)**
- Hybrid; Brill charges APCs for its open-access option. [Brill JAAE overview](https://brill.com/view/journals/jaae/jaae-overview.xml); [Brill author instructions PDF](https://brill.com/fileasset/downloads_products/Author_Instructions/JAAE.pdf) (excerpts)
- I did not verify whether the subscription route is free.

**Frontiers / MDPI**
- Both have mandatory APCs, per the brief. I did not re-verify them here.

### Inferences
- For a zero-cost author in Türkiye, the safe options are:
  - **Laboratory Animals** and **ATLA** (subscription route; skip Sage Choice)
  - **JAALAS**
  - **Laboratory Animal Research**
  - **Research Ethics**
  - **Animal Sentience**
  - **Lab Animal** (subscription route)
- **Animal Welfare** is free only if the piece is submitted as an "Article Commentary", or if an ad-hoc waiver is granted before submission. The Research4Life route does not apply to Türkiye (upper-middle-income country).
- **ALTEX** needs an institutional official to co-sign a no-funds certificate, and the waiver is still discretionary.
- **SJLAS** would cost €250 unless the author has been a Scand-LAS member for at least one year. Joining now would not help in time, and membership has its own cost.

### Gaps
- The Lab Animal APC amount and subscription-route wording come from a search excerpt; nature.com could not be fetched directly.
- I found no statement on whether ATLA or Laboratory Animals ever charge for print colour when the author has not asked for it. The pages say print colour is charged only "if requested".
- I found no author-fee statement for Derecho Animal. It is almost certainly free (a university OJS journal), but that is UNVERIFIED.
- I did not check whether Scand-LAS membership is open to non-Nordic researchers, or what it costs.
- The free subscription route of JAAE (Brill) is UNVERIFIED. So is the Journal of Animal Ethics (Univ. of Illinois Press), which I did not check.

## Q2. Scope fit and article types or word limits for a roughly 10,000-word legal comparison

### Takeaway
None of the lab-animal science journals take a 10,000-word legal paper as it stands:
- ATLA reviews are capped at 4,500 words.
- Laboratory Animals caps reviews at 7,500 words, puts original research first, and rejects reports focused on a single country.
- JAALAS lists "Opinion" and "Review" but gives no length limit in what I read.

On length and legal fit:
- **ALTEX** (no length limit) and **Derecho Animal** are the only ones that accept long legal/policy pieces, and ALTEX charges an APC.
- **Research Ethics** has a 3,000-word default, and the editors may accept longer papers.

The paper will need to be cut roughly in half for the free lab-animal journals.

### Cited Findings

**ATLA**
- Paper types: Research Papers, Review Articles, Comments, Letters, Book Reviews, Workshop/Conference Reports.
- "Articles should normally contain 2000-3000 words but Review articles may be up to 4500 words." A structured abstract of up to 250 words is required.
- The journal "does not accept any manuscripts promoting the use of animal studies."
- Source: [SAGE ATLA guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/atl)

**Laboratory Animals (LAJ)**, all from [SAGE LAN guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN):
- "LAJ prioritizes original research."
- Literature Reviews: up to 7,500 words including references. The journal prioritizes reviews "on novel methodologies or high-impact topics with regards to animal welfare and the 3Rs."
- LAS Perspectives: up to 1,500 words and 10 references.
- Working Group Reports (7,500 words) come only from subscribing societies, and "Reports with a strong and restricted focus/emphasis on a unique country will not be accepted."
- The guidelines cite Directive 2010/63/EU Annex IV and ask for "a brief statement describing the legislative controls on animal care and use", which shows the journal engages with legislation.

**JAALAS / CM**, both from [AALAS IFA](https://aalas.kglmeridian.com/view/journals/72010024/63/1/article-p99.xml):
- JAALAS accepts "Original Research (multiple categories), Review, Letters to the Editor, and Opinion." Its mission explicitly includes "compliance".
- CM accepts Case Study, Case Series, Original Research, Review and Letter.

**ALTEX**, from [ALTEX Submissions](https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/about/submissions):
- Article types include Research, Opinion ("Food for Thought…"), Concept, Review, Consensus and Workshop reports, among others.
- "There is no length restriction for these article types."
- Articles need a statement of 3R relevance, a 250-word summary and a 150-word plain-language summary.

**Animal Welfare**
- The UFAW journal page says: "Papers on ethical and legal issues…" (excerpt), meaning legal topics are in scope. [Science for Animal Welfare – Our Journal](https://scienceforanimalwelfare.org/publication/journal-animal-welfare/)
- The journal also has a "Horizon and Opinion Papers" collection. [CUP Animal Welfare](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing) (menu)
- Word limits: UNVERIFIED, because the "preparing your materials" page could not be fetched.

**Laboratory Animal Research**
- Scope includes "informed and thoughtful opinions relevant to the humane care and use of laboratory animals" alongside reviews. [Springer LAR guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/42826/submission-guidelines)

**Research Ethics**
- General submissions "no longer than 3,000 words (excluding references)… Longer manuscripts may be considered at the discretion of the Editors." [SAGE REA guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/rea) (excerpt)
- Its focus is mainly research ethics governance. Its fit with animal-experiment ethics committees (HADYEK/HADMEK) is plausible but UNVERIFIED.

**Lab Animal**
- Publishes "reviews and perspectives, research articles, methods-based columns, news and technology features". [Lab Animal content types](https://www.nature.com/laban/content) (excerpt)
- Word limits and whether reviews are commissioned: UNVERIFIED.

**Derecho Animal**
- A legal journal on animal law and interdisciplinary human–animal studies. It publishes mostly in Spanish, with selected articles in English. Review is double-blind by two external reviewers. [DA about](https://revistes.uab.cat/da/about); [Animal Legal & Historical Center listing](https://www.animallaw.info/policy/derecho-animal-archivos) (excerpts)

**Animal Sentience**
- Focus is animal feeling and cognition, with open peer commentary. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Sentience_(journal)); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/toc/2377-7478)
- A pure regulatory comparison is a weak fit (inference).

### Inferences
- **Best free fit: Laboratory Animals**, as a Review of up to 7,500 words that frames the paper as EU-harmonisation and 3Rs implementation rather than a Turkey-only report. Its "unique country" exclusion is written for Working Group Reports, but editors may apply the same thinking.
- **Second: ATLA**, as a Review of 4,500 words or a Comment.
- **Third: JAALAS**, as a Review or Opinion. Its readers are US-focused.
- **Laboratory Animal Research** (free, Scopus/ESCI status unverified) is a lower-profile but fully free OA option.
- If the full 10,000-word legal analysis must be kept, **Derecho Animal** (free, law-focused) is the natural home. **ALTEX** is only an option if a waiver is granted.

### Gaps
- Word limits for Animal Welfare and Lab Animal (pages could not be fetched).
- Word limits for JAALAS Reviews and Opinions were not in the scraped text.
- Derecho Animal's word limits and any English-language requirements were not retrieved.

## Q3. Indexing, impact and review times

### Takeaway
Few metrics were verified from primary pages:
- Laboratory Animals: Impact Factor 1.3 (5-year 1.8), from SAGE.
- JAALAS/CM: a decision "typically… within 4 weeks of being sent for review", from AALAS.
- Derecho Animal and Animal Sentience: Scopus-indexed, per the UAB news item and Wikipedia/DOAJ.

Everything else is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **Laboratory Animals**:
  - "Impact Factor: 1.3 / 5-Year Impact Factor: 1.8". [SAGE LAN page](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN)
  - Peer review is single-anonymized. The only timing information is OnlineFirst publication; no turnaround time is stated.
- **JAALAS/CM**:
  - "Whether a manuscript is accepted, requires revisions, or is rejected… typically is decided within 4 weeks of being sent for review." Revisions are due within 1 month. [AALAS IFA](https://aalas.kglmeridian.com/view/journals/72010024/63/1/article-p99.xml)
  - The 2024 JAALAS guide reports a 68% acceptance rate and 570 pages printed in 2023. [AALAS IFA excerpt](https://aalas.kglmeridian.com/view/journals/72010024/63/1/article-p99.xml)
- **ATLA**: uses OnlineFirst publication. No metrics were retrieved. [SAGE ATLA guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/atl)
- **Derecho Animal**: added to Scopus. [UAB news](https://www.uab.cat/web/detall-de-noticia/la-revista-da-derecho-animal-forum-of-animal-law-studies-ya-esta-disponible-a-scopus-1345868391959.html?noticiaid=1345845881699) (title only)
- **Animal Sentience**: indexed in DOAJ, Scopus and Crossref. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Sentience_(journal)) (secondary)
- **SJLAS**: listed in SCImago (H-index 23). [SCImago](https://www.scimagojr.com/journalsearch.php?q=27281&tip=sid) (excerpt)

### Inferences
- From general knowledge, and not verified here: ATLA, Laboratory Animals, JAALAS, CM, ALTEX, Animal Welfare and Lab Animal are all in Web of Science (SCIE). Laboratory Animal Research is SCIE/ESCI-indexed. Research Ethics is in SSCI/ESCI.
- The report writer should present these as unverified or check them separately.

### Gaps
- JCR impact factors or quartiles for ATLA, ALTEX, Animal Welfare, Lab Animal, JAALAS, CM, LAR and Research Ethics.
- Stated review times for any journal other than the AALAS journals.

## Q4. Waivers for an author in Türkiye

### Takeaway
No waiver programme applies to Türkiye automatically. Cambridge's Research4Life route excludes Türkiye. The only routes left are:
- Cambridge's ad-hoc waiver, requested at or before submission.
- ALTEX's discretionary waiver, which needs an institution to co-sign.
- A Turkish institutional read-and-publish agreement with SAGE, CUP or Springer Nature. This is relevant only for optional OA; I did not check whether any exist.

### Cited Findings
- **Animal Welfare**: the Research4Life waiver or discount applies only to eligible countries. The ad-hoc waiver is for "rare cases" of demonstrated inability to pay and must be requested at or before submission. [CUP fees page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing)
- **ALTEX**: a waiver needs a cover-letter request co-signed by an institutional official. [ALTEX Submissions](https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/about/submissions)
- **SAGE**: free or discounted OA through institutional agreements, "depending on the agreement with Sage", determined by the corresponding author's affiliation. [SAGE LAN guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN); [SAGE OA agreements](https://www.sagepub.com/open-access-agreements-msg)
- **Brill**: country-based APC waivers and discounts exist. The listed waiver countries are African; I did not confirm whether Türkiye gets a discount. [EIFL Brill APCs](https://www.eifl.net/apcs/brill-apcs-open-access-publishing) (excerpt)

### Inferences
- The author does not need a waiver for the recommended options, because they publish at no cost anyway: Laboratory Animals, ATLA, JAALAS, LAR, Research Ethics, Animal Sentience, Derecho Animal (probably), and Lab Animal (subscription route).
- Turkish institutions do have TÜBİTAK/ULAKBİM (EKUAL) read-and-publish agreements with some publishers. Whether SAGE, CUP or Springer Nature are covered in 2026 is UNVERIFIED.

### Gaps
- Whether ULAKBİM/EKUAL 2025–2026 agreements cover CUP (which would make Animal Welfare free) or SAGE/Springer Nature OA was not checked.
