# Turkish zero-fee journals for "Two Gazettes / İki Gazete" (EU Directive 2010/63/EU vs Turkish lab-animal law)

Research date: 2026-09-28. How the sources were checked: DergiPark (dergipark.org.tr), turkiyeklinikleri.com and vetjournal.ankara.edu.tr were blocked for direct fetch in this environment. I read three pages in full through a scraper: the Türkiye Biyoetik Dergisi fee page, the AÜHFD fee page and the Kafkas Vet author guidelines. The scraper then became unavailable, so every other claim comes from search-engine result summaries or snippets of the cited page. Those claims are marked **[snippet]**. Before submitting, open each journal's DergiPark `/price-policy` page (URL pattern `https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/<slug>/price-policy`) and check the "Son Güncelleme" (last-updated) date.

## Q1. Which journals verifiably charge nothing (no APC, no değerlendirme/yayın/basım ücreti, no submission fee)?

### Takeaway
At least 12 relevant Turkish journals say they charge authors no fee of any kind. Three of these were confirmed by reading the full page: Türkiye Biyoetik Dergisi, AÜHFD and Kafkas Vet. The strongest fits are the Journal of Laboratory Animal Science and Practices (Atatürk Univ.), Türkiye Biyoetik Dergisi, Turkish Journal of Veterinary & Animal Sciences (TÜBİTAK), AÜHFD, DEÜHFD and AHBVÜ HFD. The user suspected Kafkas Vet charges fees; its guidelines say it does not.

## Q2. Indexing and value for doçentlik (Turkish academic promotion)

### Takeaway
All the law journals shortlisted are TR Dizin-indexed and count as "ulusal hakemli" (national peer-reviewed) outlets under ÜAK criteria. Journals that charge fees after acceptance are excluded from doçentlik counting under a 2019 YÖK/ÜAK rule, so zero-fee journals avoid that problem entirely.

### Cited Findings
- The ÜAK decision of 7 March 2019 says journals that charge authors a fee after acceptance cannot be used to meet doçentlik requirements. Journals with no fees in the publication process remain eligible **[snippet, secondary source]**. Sources: [Akademi Mevzuat](https://www.akademimevzuat.com.tr/makaleyi-acik-erisimli-yapmak-icin-ucret-talep-eden-dergi-yagmaci-saibeli-midir/); [ÜAK başvuru şartları](https://www.uak.gov.tr/page/docentlik-basvuru-sartlari-kLPHX)
- Indexing of the candidates:
  - Kafkas Vet: WoS, Scopus, TR Dizin — [source](https://vetdergikafkas.org/static.php?id=143)
  - TJVAS: SCI-E, Scopus **[snippet]** — [source](https://journals.tubitak.gov.tr/veterinary/about.html)
  - DEÜHFD: TR Dizin, HeinOnline — [source](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/deuhfd/indexes)
  - AHBVÜ HFD: TR Dizin, DOAJ, ERIH PLUS — [source](https://doaj.org/toc/2651-4141)
  - JLASP: TR Dizin, DOAJ — [source](https://bilimseldergiler.atauni.edu.tr/journal-of-labaratory-animal-science-and-practices-dergisi-tr-dizin-kabulu/)
  - Law and Justice Review: TR Dizin — [source](https://search.trdizin.gov.tr/dergi/detay/757)
  - İstanbul Hukuk Mecmuası: TR Dizin — [source](https://search.trdizin.gov.tr/en/dergi/detay/2497)

### Inferences
- A veterinary or health-sciences author gets the most doçentlik points from a WoS/Scopus veterinary journal (TJVAS, Kafkas, Ankara Vet). A law or social-sciences author benefits from TR Dizin law journals (AÜHFD, DEÜHFD), with HeinOnline and ESCI adding weight. The best choice depends on the author's doçentlik field.

## Q3. Do TR Dizin or DergiPark require journals to publish their fees?

### Takeaway
Yes. TR Dizin criteria require journals to state their fee and fee policy, if any, under the publication-policy section of their website. DergiPark gives every journal a standard "Ücret Politikası" (`/price-policy`) page, so a missing or blank page is itself a warning sign.

### Cited Findings
- TR Dizin evaluation criteria require journals to publish open-access information, "varsa ücret ve ücretlendirme politikası", and review-process details on the journal website **[snippet]**. Sources: [TR Dizin criteria FAQ](https://trdizin.gov.tr/ufaq/tr-dizin-dergi-degerlendirme-kriterleri-nelerdir/); [Criteria](https://trdizin.gov.tr/en/criteria/)
- DergiPark hosts a standard price-policy page per journal. Examples: [turbietderg](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/turbietderg/price-policy), [auhfd](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/auhfd/price-policy), [ahbvuhfd](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/ahbvuhfd/price-policy).
- Some DergiPark journals do charge. For example, Tekirdağ Ziraat charged until 2021, when it announced "Mayıs 2021 ... itibaren yayın sürecinde herhangi bir ücret alınmayacaktır". Being on DergiPark does not by itself mean a journal is free. [JOTAF](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/jotaf)

### Inferences
- The practical check is to open `https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/<slug>/price-policy` and look for "Hiçbir ad altında yazar veya kurumundan ücret alınmaz" (the DergiPark default free-text wording) and the last-updated date.

### Gaps
- I did not find a DergiPark-wide rule (as opposed to TR Dizin's) that forbids or requires fees.
