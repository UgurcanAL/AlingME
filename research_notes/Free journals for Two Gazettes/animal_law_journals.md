# Zero-cost journals for "Two Gazettes" (EU Directive 2010/63/EU vs Turkish lab-animal law): animal-law, animal-ethics, EU/comparative-law journals, 2025–2026

Method note (research conditions, 28 Sep 2026): Direct page fetches were mostly blocked by the network egress proxy (WebFetch returned EGRESS_BLOCKED for journal.fi, revistes.uab.cat, doaj.org, journals.lub.lu.se, press.uillinois.edu, law.lclark.edu), and Firecrawl scraping only worked twice before its permission check started failing. **Verification level is marked on each item:**
- **[PAGE]** = I read the official page myself (full text scraped).
- **[SNIPPET]** = taken from a search-engine summary of the official page named in the URL. The URL is the official page, but I did not read the full page. The author should open it and confirm before submitting.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** = no official source found.

## Q1. Which animal-law and animal-ethics journals are truly zero-cost (no APC, no submission fee, no page charges) and fit a ~10,000-word EU–Turkey lab-animal regulatory comparison?

### Takeaway
The best zero-cost fits are **dA. Derecho Animal (UAB)**, which is diamond OA, indexed in Scopus, accepts English and allows articles of 8,000–12,000 words; the **Global Journal of Animal Law (GJAL, Åbo Akademi / journal.fi)**, which is OA with a limit of about 10,000 words; and **LEOH (Journal of Animal Law, Ethics and One Health, University of Zurich)**, which is diamond OA with articles of about 10,000 words on average and no strict limit. The US student-edited animal-law reviews (Lewis & Clark *Animal Law*, MSU *Animal and Natural Resource Law Review*) are also free to publish in. They accept work from non-students, but the manuscript would need to be reformatted in Bluebook style.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- For a ~10,000-word article, **dA (8,000–12,000 words)** has the most room, and **GJAL and LEOH (about 10,000 words)** fit if the text is kept at or under 10k. JAEE (8,000 words) and Journal of Animal Ethics (3,000–5,000 words normally) would require substantial cuts.
- Citation style differs by journal: GJAL uses OSCOLA, dA uses Chicago NB, US law reviews use Bluebook, and J Animal Ethics uses APA 7. Whichever journal comes first will need a re-format.
- Politics and Animals, JCAS and AJFP are free, but their political-theory or critical-studies orientation fits a doctrinal regulatory comparison less well.
- The hybrid journals (Society & Animals, JAEE) are free only on the subscription route. They are acceptable if the author just avoids selecting OA.
- The US student law reviews accept submissions from outside the US and from authors who are not students ("scholarly works ... of any length"), but they tend to prefer US-relevant analysis. A purely EU–Turkey comparison is a weaker fit there than at GJAL, dA or LEOH.

## Q2. Which EU-law, comparative-law and Turkish journals would publish an EU-candidate-country regulatory-alignment study at zero cost?

### Takeaway
The Turkish DergiPark journals are the safest zero-cost EU-alignment venues. **Law and Justice Review (Justice Academy of Türkiye)** states that no fee is charged at any stage and publishes in English. **Ankara Avrupa Çalışmaları Dergisi** states no submission, processing or printing fees. **Marmara Journal of European Studies** fits the topic well, but its fee page is unverified. Of the European journals, **REALaw** has no submission fee on the standard (delayed-OA) route. **EJRR** is now fully gold OA with a US$3,550 APC, so it is free only if Cambridge grants a waiver and is **not** reliably zero-cost.

### Cited Findings

**Law and Justice Review (Justice Academy of Türkiye), DergiPark `ljr`**
- The journal is international, peer-reviewed, open access and published twice yearly (January and July) since 2010. It publishes in English, French or German, with double-blind review. "No fee can be charged at any stage". Contact: lawandjusticereview@taa.gov.tr. [SNIPPET] — [LJR DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ljr); [TAA ethics page](https://lawandjustice.taa.gov.tr/sayfa/law-etik-ilkeler)
- Word limit and indexing are **unverified**.

**Ankara Avrupa Çalışmaları Dergisi / Ankara Review of European Studies (Ankara University EC Research Centre), DergiPark `aacd`**
- The journal takes no submission, processing or printing fee, because Ankara University covers the costs. It has been published twice yearly (June and December) since 2001, in Turkish and English, and is peer-reviewed. Submission is via DergiPark together with an authorship and copyright form. [SNIPPET] — [AAÇD DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/aacd); [AAÇD site](https://aacd.ankara.edu.tr/en/mainpage/)

- It has been published since 1991, twice yearly (June and December), in Turkish and English, with double-blind review. Its focus is European integration and Turkey's political, economic and legal position, including law. It is indexed in EconLit, EBSCO Academic Search Complete and ASOS. [SNIPPET] — [MJES About](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/maruaad/about-journal)
- Fees: **[UNVERIFIED]**. The price-policy page was not retrieved. Check https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/maruaad (look for its "Price Policy" tab).

**Review of European Administrative Law (REALaw), Paris Legal Publishers, edited at Groningen and Utrecht**
- The journal is peer-reviewed and published quarterly. It has **no submission fee**. Articles become OA automatically after two years, and immediate OA requires a fee, which is optional. Scope: "all aspects of European administrative law ... the role of domestic legal orders and their mutual relation and influence." [SNIPPET] — [REALaw at Uitgeverij Paris](https://www.uitgeverijparis.nl/en/tijdschriften-online/review-of-european-administrative-law-realaw); [Open Policy Finder](https://openpolicyfinder.jisc.ac.uk/id/publication/20860)

[SNIPPET] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Policy_Quarterly) (secondary); [TPQ site](http://turkishpolicy.com/guide-to-book-review-contributors)

**"Journal of European Integration Studies"**
- **[UNVERIFIED]**. I could not identify a specific Turkish or European journal by this exact name.

### Inferences
- LJR and AAÇD are clearly zero-cost and accept English. They would likely welcome an EU-acquis alignment comparison. LJR's legal-doctrinal profile makes it the stronger fit for a comparison of penalties and inspection.
- REALaw fits the inspection, authorisation and transparency sections as administrative law, and it costs nothing if the author does not buy immediate OA. The paper would need to be framed around administrative-law mechanisms, such as project authorisation as an administrative act, ethics committees, and inspection.
- EJRR should be treated as **not zero-cost** unless a waiver is confirmed before submission. An author based in Türkiye may or may not qualify for Cambridge's equity waivers, and eligibility needs checking.

- I did not check whether Turkish ULAKBİM TR Dizin indexing applies.

## Q3. Summary ranking for a zero-cost submission (fit × certainty of zero cost)

### Takeaway
Suggested order: (1) dA. Derecho Animal, (2) GJAL, (3) LEOH, then (4) Law and Justice Review or AAÇD as EU-alignment alternatives, with (5) REALaw on the non-OA route. Lewis & Clark *Animal Law Review* is a free US option if the paper is Bluebooked. Avoid ALTEX (€2,500 APC) and EJRR (US$3,550 APC unless waived).

### Cited Findings
- dA: Scopus-indexed, diamond, English accepted, 8,000–12,000 words. — [dA Submissions](https://revistes.uab.cat/da/about/submissions)
- GJAL: OA, ≤10,000 words, OSCOLA, double-blind. — [GJAL Submissions](https://journal.fi/gjal/about/submissions)
- LEOH: diamond OA, about 10,000 words, English/German/French. — [LEOH Submissions](https://leoh.ch/about/submissions)
- LJR: no fee at any stage, English. — [LJR DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ljr)
- AAÇD: no submission, processing or printing fee. — [AAÇD DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/aacd)
- REALaw: no submission fee, delayed OA. — [Uitgeverij Paris](https://www.uitgeverijparis.nl/en/tijdschriften-online/review-of-european-administrative-law-realaw)
- ALTEX APC €2,500 — [ALTEX About](https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/about); EJRR APC US$3,550 — [EJRR fees](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-journal-of-risk-regulation/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing)

### Inferences
- A ~10,000+ word manuscript would need trimming to ≤10k for GJAL and LEOH. dA accepts it as it is, up to 12k.
- The journals prohibit simultaneous submission (dA, J Animal Ethics and GJAL state this explicitly), so submissions must be sequential.

### Gaps
- Review turnaround times are unknown for most of these journals. Only J Animal Ethics (aims for ≤3 months) and JAEE (about 3 months average) state one.
