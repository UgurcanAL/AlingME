# Send "Two Gazettes" to animal-law journals first

**Recommendation: submit the English version of "Two Gazettes" first to *dA. Derecho Animal* (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona).** It is indexed in Scopus, it is diamond open access (free to read and free to publish), it publishes in English, and its article band of 8,000–12,000 words fits a ~10,000-word paper without cuts ([dA Submissions](https://revistes.uab.cat/da/about/submissions)). If dA declines, try these in order:
1. The *Global Journal of Animal Law* (GJAL), with a 10,000-word cap and OSCOLA citations.
2. *LEOH*, the University of Zurich's animal-law journal, with about 10,000 words and no strict limit.
3. *Laboratory Animals* (SAGE). This is the strongest zero-cost lab-animal-science option, and the only international one whose "no fees payable" statement was read on the full official page. It forces a cut to a **7,500-word review, with references counted inside that limit** ([SAGE LAN guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN)).
4. Turkish outlets. The safest are *Ankara Üniversitesi Hukuk Fakültesi Dergisi* (AÜHFD) and *Türkiye Biyoetik Dergisi*, both with free-of-charge wording confirmed on the full page. The best topical fit is the *Journal of Laboratory Animal Science and Practices* (JLASP), and *Law and Justice Review* takes English only.

The journals to avoid are those that sound on-topic but charge:
- ALTEX: €2,500 APC.
- *Animal Welfare* (UFAW/Cambridge): £2,610 APC.
- *European Journal of Risk Regulation*: US$3,550 APC.
- Scandinavian Journal of Laboratory Animal Science: €250 for non-members.

Every hybrid journal is free only if you do not tick the paid open-access box. The main caution is verification. The research team could not open most official pages directly because of a proxy, so several "no fee" claims, including dA's, rest on search-engine excerpts of the official page. Check each one on the live fee page, and note its date, before uploading.

## Legal journals accept a 10,000-word paper; lab-animal journals want half

The paper's biggest constraint is not cost but genre. It is a doctrinal comparison of statutes, regulations and committee structures. The lab-animal science journals that are genuinely free all cap reviews well below 10,000 words and put original research first:
- ATLA: "Articles should normally contain 2000-3000 words but Review articles may be up to 4500 words," and a structured 250-word abstract is required ([SAGE ATLA guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/atl)).
- *Laboratory Animals*: literature reviews "up to 7,500 words including references". It prioritises reviews "on novel methodologies or high-impact topics with regards to animal welfare and the 3Rs". Its Working Group Report category states that reports "with a strong and restricted focus/emphasis on a unique country will not be accepted" ([SAGE LAN guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN)). That exclusion formally covers only Working Group Reports, but an editor could read a Turkey-centred review the same way. The paper would need to be framed as a case study of how a candidate country transposes Directive 2010/63/EU, not as a report on Türkiye.

Animal-law journals were built for exactly this length and format:
- **dA** asks for articles of "mínimo 8.000 a 12.000 palabras". Its citation examples even include EU Commission documents and Spain's Ley 32/2007 on experimental animals ([dA Submissions](https://revistes.uab.cat/da/about/submissions)).
- **GJAL** asks that articles "normally not be longer than 10,000 words", with a ~250-word abstract ([GJAL Submissions](https://journal.fi/gjal/about/submissions)). Its stated scope is that "no topic relevant to animal law is excluded" ([GJAL About](https://journal.fi/gjal/about)).
- **LEOH** reports a 10,000-word average for articles with "no strict limit", and accepts English, German and French ([LEOH Submissions](https://leoh.ch/about/submissions)).

Turkish law faculty reviews also routinely take long articles. *TBB Dergisi* allows up to about 12,000 words or 30 journal pages ([TBB rules](http://portal.ubap.org.tr/TbbDergisi/kurallar.aspx)).

The trade-off is therefore clear. Choose a law journal and the paper survives almost intact, apart from a citation-style change. Choose a lab-animal journal and you gain an Impact Factor audience (*Laboratory Animals* reports IF 1.3, 5-year 1.8 ([SAGE LAN](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN))), but you must cut 25–55% and recast the argument around 3Rs and welfare outcomes. Because only one language version may be submitted, and journals such as dA and GJAL explicitly prohibit simultaneous submission, submissions must go out one at a time.

## Five on-topic journals charge and should be skipped

The biggest traps are the journals whose scope fits best. **ALTEX** bills "€2,500 per full manuscript… and €1,300 per short communication upon acceptance", plus €90 per printed page for letters. Its waiver needs a cover-letter request "co-signed by an institutional official certifying that the authors do not have funds available" and remains discretionary ("may") (**FULL**, [ALTEX Submissions](https://www.altex.org/index.php/altex/about/submissions)).

**Animal Welfare** (UFAW, now Cambridge gold OA) charges **£2,610 / US$3,655** (**FULL**, [CUP fees page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing)). Several escape routes exist, none reliable:
- It waives APCs for Editorials, Letters, **Article Commentaries** and Book Reviews. A heavily condensed commentary would be free, but the full paper would not.
- The Research4Life waiver does not cover Türkiye.
- The ad-hoc waiver is for "rare cases" and must be requested "at or before submission".
- Whether a Turkish ULAKBİM/EKUAL read-and-publish agreement covers Cambridge in 2026 was not checked. That is worth one email to the author's library.

The other three charging journals rest on search excerpts:
- **EJRR** has been fully gold OA since December 2024 at **US$3,550** (**SNIPPET**, [EJRR fees](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-journal-of-risk-regulation/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing)).
- **SJLAS** is not the free diamond journal it is sometimes described as. It charges "250 € for an accepted manuscript" unless the corresponding author has been a Scand-LAS member for at least a year (**SNIPPET**, [SJLAS Submissions](https://ojs.utlib.ee/index.php/SJLAS/about/submissions)).
- **Lab Animal's** OA route costs £3,990 / $5,590 / €4,790 (**SNIPPET**, [Lab Animal GTA](https://www.nature.com/laban/authors-and-referees/gta)).

Frontiers and MDPI titles have mandatory APCs. The research team did not re-verify those amounts.

Hybrid journals are safe only if you decline their paid option at acceptance: Sage Choice (ATLA, *Laboratory Animals*), Brill Open (*Society & Animals*, *Journal of Applied Animal Ethics Research*), Springer OA (*Journal of Agricultural and Environmental Ethics*) and REALaw's immediate OA ([SAGE LAN](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/LAN); [Brill SOAN](https://brill.com/fileasset/downloads_products/Author_Instructions/SOAN.pdf); [JAEE](https://link.springer.com/journal/10806/submission-guidelines)).

In Türkiye, Türkiye Klinikleri sells a paid "Yayın Destek" manuscript-preparation service. It is not required, and you should not buy it ([Yayın Destek](https://www.turkiyeklinikleri.com/yayindestek)). Its *Laboratuvar Hayvanları Dergisi* has no fee statement and appears dormant since 2017 ([last issue](https://www.turkiyeklinikleri.com/journal/laboratuvar-hayvanlari-dergisi/551/last-issue/tr-index.html)). The *Stanford Journal of Animal Law & Policy* has been on hiatus since 2014 ([Stanford](https://law.stanford.edu/stanford-journal-of-animal-law-and-policy-sjalp/)).

Zero cost also protects the paper's value for promotion. A secondary source reports that a 7 March 2019 ÜAK decision bars journals that charge authors after acceptance from counting toward doçentlik ([Akademi Mevzuat](https://www.akademimevzuat.com.tr/makaleyi-acik-erisimli-yapmak-icin-ucret-talep-eden-dergi-yagmaci-saibeli-midir/)).

## Submission order and pre-submission checklist

The order below rewards fit first and certainty of zero cost second. It assumes the **English** version is the one released. Submitting "İki Gazete" to a Turkish journal would spend the paper's single language slot. Choose that path only if a TR Dizin law publication serves the author's doçentlik field (Hukuk) better than a Scopus animal-law article.

| Step | Journal | Version and edit needed | Why here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st choice | **dA. Derecho Animal** | English, ~10,000 words as is. Chicago NB; add Spanish title and abstract | Scopus-indexed, diamond, fits the length, and the core readership is animal law |
| Fallback 1 | **GJAL** | English, trimmed to ≤10,000 words, OSCOLA | Dedicated animal-law journal with no excluded topics |
| Fallback 2 | **LEOH** | English, ~10,000 words | Diamond OA, law plus ethics plus One Health |
| Fallback 3 | **Laboratory Animals** | English review ≤7,500 words including references, reframed around 3Rs and EU harmonisation | Fee-free status verified on the full page, IF 1.3, reaches lab-animal practitioners |
| Fallback 4 | **Law and Justice Review** (English) *or* **AÜHFD / JLASP** (Turkish) | English full text to LJR; or switch languages only after withdrawing all English submissions | Guaranteed-free domestic home with TR Dizin value |

Before each upload, run these checks:

| # | Check | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Re-read the fee page on the day you submit and record its last-updated date | For DergiPark, open `dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/<slug>/price-policy`, look for "Hiçbir ad altında… ücret alınmaz", and note the "Son Güncelleme" date. For the others, use the journal's fees or submissions page. Screenshot it. TR Dizin requires journals to publish any fee policy, so a missing page is a red flag ([TR Dizin criteria](https://trdizin.gov.tr/en/criteria/)) |
| 2 | Upgrade every **SNIPPET** claim to verified yourself | Most urgently dA's "no APC", GJAL's fee policy, LEOH's OA page and JLASP's language policy |
| 3 | Confirm there are no charges at any stage | Submission, evaluation (değerlendirme), acceptance, page, colour or printing. Choose online-only or greyscale figures so the "colour in print if requested" clauses never trigger |
| 4 | Decline all paid optional OA | Do not tick Sage Choice, Brill Open or Springer OA at acceptance, and ignore licence-upgrade upsells |
| 5 | Do not buy paid services | No "yayın destek", editing, translation or fast-track packages. None is required by any journal listed |
| 6 | Release one language version only | Do not submit the Turkish and English versions in parallel or sequentially to different journals. State in the cover letter that no other version is under review |
| 7 | Submit to one journal at a time | dA, GJAL and J Animal Ethics explicitly forbid simultaneous submission |
| 8 | Reformat to house style | Chicago NB (dA), OSCOLA (GJAL), Bluebook (US reviews), SAGE/Vancouver-style (LAN) |
| 9 | Anonymise the file | All the law journals use double-blind review. Remove the author's name from metadata and any self-references |
| 10 | Confirm current indexing | Check Scopus and TR Dizin listings for 2026 ([TR Dizin search](https://search.trdizin.gov.tr/)) and the ÜAK criteria for your field ([ÜAK](https://www.uak.gov.tr/page/docentlik-basvuru-sartlari-kLPHX)) |

## Conclusion

A regulatory comparison this long belongs in an animal-law journal. The zero-cost constraint barely narrows that field: every serious animal-law venue found (dA, GJAL, LEOH, the US law reviews) is diamond or subscription-free. The fee traps sit in the lab-animal and welfare-science space, which is where a paper about Directive 2010/63/EU would instinctively be sent. That inverts the usual trade-off. Staying in law costs almost nothing in cuts or money. Moving to lab-animal science brings an Impact Factor but means cutting a quarter to half of the paper and managing hybrid-OA upsells.

The unresolved risk is evidential rather than financial. Only a handful of fee statements were read on full official pages: *Laboratory Animals*, ALTEX, *Animal Welfare*, AÜHFD, Türkiye Biyoetik Dergisi and Kafkas Vet. The first-choice journal's "no APC" rests on an excerpt. A five-minute check of dA's live About page, and of each fallback's fee page, is the last step before the paper goes out.
